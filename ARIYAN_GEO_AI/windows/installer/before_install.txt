ARIYAN GEO AI - Scientific Geospatial Laboratory

What this installs
  The program, for you only (no administrator rights needed), in
  %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\ARIYAN GEO AI, with a Start-menu entry, an
  optional desktop icon and an uninstaller (Settings > Apps).

What it never touches
  - Your project data: %USERPROFILE%\ARIYAN_GEO_AI\offline_data
    (or the data folder you chose in the program)
  - Your settings, saved keys and Google Drive sign-in:
    %APPDATA%\ARIYAN_GEO_AI
  Installing a newer version updates the program in place; uninstalling
  removes only the program. Your projects, reports and keys stay.

After installing
  Open ARIYAN GEO AI. The Scientific Steward welcomes you on Mission
  Control and guides every job step by step. If this computer has no
  project yet, use "Receive from phone" or "Google Drive" first.

Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" because this installer is
not signed by a paid certificate: press "More info", then "Run anyway".
